## Code style

- Always use braces for `if`, `else`, `for`, `for...of`, `for...in`, `while`, and `do...while` bodies, including single-statement bodies.
- Put block bodies on separate lines; do not use single-line control-flow blocks.
- Use blank lines to separate logical steps: after guard clauses, between setup and execution, and around distinct loops or phases.
- Keep closely related declarations and operations together. Do not insert blank lines mechanically after every statement.
- Apply these rules to new and modified handwritten code. Do not hand-edit generated or minified files.
